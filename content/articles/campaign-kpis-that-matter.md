A useful KPI helps you make a decision. If a number changes and nobody knows what to do differently, it probably does not deserve top billing in your campaign report.

## Start with the business outcome

For a lead-generation campaign, qualified leads and cost per qualified lead are usually more important than raw impressions. For ecommerce, revenue, conversion rate, acquisition cost, and contribution margin may matter more.

## Use diagnostic metrics underneath

Metrics like CTR, CPC, bounce behavior, and landing-page conversion rate are valuable because they help explain *why* the primary outcome changed.

## Keep the scorecard small

A good campaign scorecard often needs only a handful of metrics:

- Primary conversions
- Cost per conversion
- Revenue or conversion value
- Conversion rate
- One or two channel-specific diagnostic metrics

The purpose of reporting is clarity, not decoration.
